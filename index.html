Baking conversions

Volume conversions use US customary measures. Grams and ounces are weight measurements; converting cups to grams depends on the ingredient.

Common baking weights

Approximate weights for 1 US cup: all-purpose flour 120 g · granulated sugar 200 g · packed brown sugar 220 g · butter 227 g. For accurate baking, weigh ingredients when possible.

Backup & restore

Recipes are stored on this iPhone, not synced to other devices. Export a backup regularly and save it to Files or iCloud Drive. Removing the app or clearing website data can erase locally saved recipes.
